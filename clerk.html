Clerk's Desk

Turn an approved filing into a public case entry. This page only writes text for you to paste. It cannot publish anything by itself.

  1. Check the filing from the Discord. Everything you publish is public.
  2. Fill in the form below. The next reference number is picked for you.
  3. Copy the entry, then open cases.json in GitHub.
  4. Paste it straight after the first [ at the very top, then click Commit changes. The site updates in a minute or two.
  5. Reply to the filer with their reference number.
