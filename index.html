About the court

The Narellan Federal Court settles disputes between players and rules on matters referred by server staff. Its rulings are final once the appeal window closes.

  • Civil claimsUnpaid trades, broken contracts, lost or borrowed items.
  • Property disputesOverlapping land claims, griefed builds, and ownership of registered lots.
  • Rule violationsCases referred by moderators where a player disputes the charge.
  • AppealsReview of any earlier ruling by the full bench.
